# AFTER THREE — treatment

**Logline.** A love story told only by the things in the room. One apartment,
one night, 03:07 AM to noon. We never see a face. The keys, the glass, a button,
the couch and the light tell what happened.

## Visual language

* **Objects, not people.** Every shot is a close, intimate look at an object
  that carries a line of the lyric: keys, the glass rinsed "once again", a
  zipper, a shirt button, a belt buckle, a strand of hair, the clock, the couch.
  This keeps the video tasteful while the song gets more physical. The
  audience's imagination does the rest.
* **Light tells the time.** Night is tungsten amber (the pendant lamp over the
  counter) against cold blue moonlight through the blinds. A red neon sign
  across the street wakes up in the choruses and pulses with the kick drum.
  The bridge is moonlight only. Verse 3 is a hard cut to warm, high-key noon.
* **The count.** "One, two, three" is the song's hook, so numerals are the
  typographic motif: huge serif numerals behind the chorus shots, a slow and
  drifting count in the bridge ("count too slow"), numerals spilling past four
  when the count is lost.
* **Lyrics as typography.** Instrument Serif italic, revealed word by word on
  the sung timing (word timings were taken from the vocal itself by speech
  recognition on an isolated vocal stem).
* **Finish.** Physically based 3D lighting, depth of field, bloom with a warm
  halation, filmic tone mapping, per-section colour grade, chromatic aberration,
  vignette, animated grain, 2.39:1 letterbox.
